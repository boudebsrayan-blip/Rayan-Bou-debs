# Meatus: Business Notes

A summary of everything we planned so far for adding ready-to-cook products to the butchery.

## The shop

- **Name:** Meatus ("meat" + "us": the beef and lamb, and the family behind it)
- **Sells:** beef and lamb only
- **Location:** Lebanon
- **Today:** customers come in and choose their meat at the counter
- **Idea:** add ready-to-cook packs that customers take from the fridge and only need to cook

## Product ideas

### Beef
Kefta, kefta skewers, burger patties, meatballs, beef shawarma strips, beef skewers, marinated steaks, breaded escalopes, stew packs, stuffed vegetables, beef merguez

### Lamb
Lamb merguez, marinated chops, lamb skewers, marinated leg of lamb, marinated shoulder, lamb kefta, lamb shawarma, tagine or stew packs, marinated ribs

### Starting set
1. Beef kefta
2. Lamb merguez
3. Mixed skewers (beef and lamb)
4. Burger patties
5. Marinated lamb chops
6. Beef stew or tagine pack

## Skewers and ribeye: the plan

- **Start with skewers:** cheap to test, high margin, popular for mashawi, and a good use for trimmings.
- **Ribeye as a small premium item:** 2–3 packs a day, or pre-orders on WhatsApp, so expensive meat isn't wasted.
- **Week 1–2:** beef, lamb and kefta skewers, about 5 packs of each per day.
- **Week 3:** add 2–3 ribeye packs and post them on Instagram or WhatsApp.
- **Week 4:** keep what sells and drop what doesn't.
- **Watch:** shorter shelf life, fridge space, hygiene, and daily sales records.

## Pricing: 1 kg beef skewer pack

1 kg of meat in each pack (not counting the weight of the packaging).

| Cost item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Beef, 1 kg (what we pay) | $15.00 |
| Skewers | $0.12 |
| Pack | $0.84 |
| Marinade, onion, pepper *(estimate)* | about $0.60 |
| Label and film *(estimate)* | about $0.15 |
| **Total cost** | **about $16.70** |

| | Raw beef (1 kg) | Skewer pack (1 kg) |
|---|---|---|
| Selling price | $20 | **$24** |
| Cost | $15 | about $16.70 |
| **Profit** | **$5.00** | **$7.30** |
| Margin | 25% | 30% |

- **Final price: $24**, which is $4 more than raw beef.
- **Extra profit vs. selling raw:** $2.30 per pack. At 10 packs a day, that's about $23 a day, or around $690 a month.
- **Launch:** $22 in week 1, then $24. Try $25 later if packs sell out every day.
- **Minimum price:** don't go below $21.
- **Check:** if cutting the cubes loses meat to trimming (about 1.1 kg raw for 1 kg of clean cubes), the real cost is closer to $18.20, so stay at $24 or go to $25.

## BBQ boxes

- **Duo:** 2 people, about 1.1 kg: 2 beef skewers, 2 lamb skewers, 2 kefta skewers, 4 merguez, 2 lamb chops
- **Family:** 4–5 people, about 2.3 kg: 4 beef skewers, 4 lamb skewers, 8 merguez, 4 kefta skewers, 4 lamb chops
- **Party:** 8–10 people, about 4.6 kg: double the Family box
- **Layout:** skewers on the left, a parsley line in the middle, merguez, kefta and chops on the right, lemon wedges in the corner
- **On top:** label, spice sachet, cooking card
