Packing order

  1. Put an absorbent pad in the bottom of the tray so marinade and juices don't pool.
  2. Lay the beef skewers (1) in the top left, all pointing the same way, sticks toward the outside edge.
  3. Lay the lamb skewers (2) under them, same direction. Keeping beef and lamb in separate groups makes them easy to tell apart.
  4. Run a line of fresh parsley down the middle as a divider.
  5. On the right side, place the merguez (3) side by side across the top.
  6. Place the kefta skewers (4) in the middle row.
  7. Fan the lamb chops (5) in the bottom row, bones pointing up, so each one shows.
  8. Add 2 lemon wedges in the corner, then seal with film or a clear lid.
  9. Stick the label on the lid and tape the spice sachet and cooking card on top.
