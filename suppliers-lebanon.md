# Meatus: Packaging Suppliers in Lebanon

Notes for buying packaging for the BBQ box. These leads come from business directories and company websites. Call before visiting to check what they sell, their prices, and whether they sell small quantities.

## 1. Shopping list for the 10-box test

| Item | Where to buy | How many |
|---|---|---|
| Black trays with clear lids, about 30 × 20 cm | Disposables shop, or the takeaway section of Carrefour or Spinneys | 10 |
| Cling film | Any supermarket | 1 roll |
| Absorbent pads | Ask a supermarket meat counter or another butcher; kitchen paper works for the test | 10 |
| Wooden skewers, 25 cm | Supermarket or disposables shop | 1 pack |
| A4 white sticker paper for labels | Stationery or print shop (maktaba) | 1–2 sheets |
| Small zip bags for spice | Supermarket or disposables shop | 10 |

**Tip:** to find a disposables shop near you, ask a shawarma or snack restaurant where they buy their boxes.

### What to say when you call

> "I need 10 black rectangular trays with clear lids, around 30 × 20 cm, for raw meat. Do you sell small quantities, or can you give me samples?"

Mention that you're testing a new product before ordering in bulk. Companies often give free samples.

### Questions to ask

1. Do you sell black meat trays about 36 × 26 cm? What's the price per 100?
2. Do you have absorbent pads for meat?
3. What's the minimum order?
4. Do you deliver to my area?
