# 70-Year-Old Client – High-Intensity Circuits

**Client:** 70 years old, loves high-intensity training, elbow injury.
**Room equipment:** rack, bench, barbell, dumbbells, plates, pendulum squat, sled.

**Format:** Circuits 1–3: 3 rounds each, minimal rest between exercises, 90 sec rest after each round.
Circuit 4: 10-minute AMRAP. Total about 45–50 min.

| Circuit | Exercise | Reps / Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Warm-up** (6 min) | Bodyweight squat to bench, arm circles, band pull-apart | 2 rounds | |
| **Circuit 1: Squat + Sled** | **Pendulum squat**, moderate-heavy | 10 | Controlled down, strong up |
| | **Sled push**, heavy | 20 m | Arms straight, push with the legs |
| | **Landmine press**, half-kneeling or standing | 8 each arm | Barbell end in the corner of the rack |
| **Circuit 2: Hinge + Pull** | **Dumbbell Romanian deadlift** *(straps)* | 10 | Hips back, flat back |
| | **Chest-supported dumbbell row** *(straps, neutral grip)* | 12 | Pain-free weight |
| | **Decline sit-up** on bench | 12 | Arms crossed, weighted vest to progress |
| **Circuit 3: Push + Legs** | **Bench press**, moderate | 10 | Pain-free, no grinding |
| | **Wall sit** with weighted vest | 40 sec | Thighs parallel |
| | **Hollow body hold** | 20–30 sec | Lower back pressed down; bend knees to make it easier |
| **Circuit 4: Finisher – 10-min AMRAP** | **Dumbbell thruster** (squat into overhead press) | 8 | Moderate dumbbells, one smooth movement |
| | **Alternating dumbbell snatch** | 10 total | Floor to overhead, switch arms |
| | **Shuttle run** across the room | 4 lengths | Fast pace, slow down before each turn, no sharp pivots |
| **Cool-down** (3 min) | Walk + stretching | – | |

**AMRAP score:** record rounds completed and try to beat it next session.

## Elbow
- Straps on the Romanian deadlift and rows; neutral grips.
- If bench press or landmine press hurts: neutral-grip dumbbell press or lighter weight.
- If the snatch hurts: replace with 10 more light thrusters, or 2 extra shuttle lengths.
- No curls, triceps extensions, dips, pull-ups or heavy gripping. Pain must stay at 3/10 or lower.

## Safety (age 70)
- Effort around 8/10, no breath holding on lifts.
- He can rest briefly in the AMRAP; the clock keeps running.
- Stop immediately with dizziness, chest pain or unusual shortness of breath.
- Make sure he has medical clearance for high-intensity training.
