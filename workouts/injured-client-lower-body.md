# Injured Client – Lower Body Strength

**Restrictions:** shoulder injury (doctor: no shoulder use) + torn meniscus.
No shoulder use at all, no deep knee bending, no twisting, no jumping or running.

| Block | Exercise | Sets × Reps | Rest |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Warm-up** | Bike (high seat, no hands) | 6 min | – |
| | Band terminal knee extension (no hands) | 2 × 15 | – |
| | Glute bridge, bodyweight | 2 × 12 | – |
| **A1** | 45° back extension, glute focus (arms relaxed, not crossed on chest) | 4 × 10 | – |
| **A2** | Banded lateral walk (band above knees, half-squat stance) | 3 × 12 steps each way | 90 sec |
| **B1** | Leg press, partial range (about 0–70° knee bend) | 4 × 10 | – |
| **B2** | Standing calf raise machine (belt or hip version) *or* seated calf raise | 4 × 12 | 90 sec |
| **C1** | Hip thrust machine *(or glute bridge with plate placed on hips by coach)* | 4 × 10 | – |
| **C2** | Leg extension, short range (about 60° → straight) | 3 × 12 | 90 sec |
| **D1** | Single-leg leg press, partial range | 3 × 10 each leg | – |
| **D2** | Low step-up (15–20 cm box, slow, no hands) | 3 × 8 each leg | 60–90 sec |
| **E1** | Hamstring isometric bridge (heels on box, hold) | 3 × 20 sec | – |
| **E2** | Straight leg raise with ankle weight | 3 × 12 each leg | 60 sec |
| **Finisher** | Incline treadmill walk, no rails | 8–10 min | – |

Supersets: do exercise 1, go straight into exercise 2, then rest.

## Coaching notes
- **Load:** finish each set with 2–3 reps in reserve. Add weight week to week only if the knee stays pain-free and doesn't swell the next day.
- **Knee:** 3-second lowering, knees in line with toes, no twisting on step-ups.
- **Shoulder:** coach sets up all weights (plates, machine adjustments, plate on hips). Client doesn't grip handles hard or push up with his arms. Help him on and off the back extension.
- **Back extension:** to progress, use a weighted vest instead of holding a plate.
- **Lateral walk:** small controlled steps, knees pushed out, no twisting.
- **Stop** any exercise that causes sharp pain, catching, locking or swelling.
