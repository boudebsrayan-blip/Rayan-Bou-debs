Meatus

Meat is the beef and lamb. Us is the family behind the counter. The mark is a bold M: one peak curls into a ram's horn for lamb, and the other rises into a bull's horn for beef.

Versions

Round stamp, for stickers, bags and the shop window
Stacked, for signs, the BBQ box lid and menus
Icon only, for Instagram, WhatsApp and the website

The idea

  • One letter, both animals. The two horns show what you sell, beef and lamb, without needing a picture of a cow or a sheep.
  • "US" in red. It splits the name into meat and us and points to the family and the customers.
  • Slab letters. Heavy, square-footed type, like old butcher shop signs and cleaver stamps. It reads as strong and traditional.
  • Three colors only. Easy and cheap to print on stickers, bags, trays and aprons.

On your products

A heads-up on the name: in English, "meatus" is also a medical word for a body opening. Most customers won't know it, and as one word next to the horn logo it reads clearly as meat + us. If you want to avoid it, "Meat & Us" or "MeatUs" with the capital U keeps the same idea.
